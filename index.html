Samson Solar Energy

Karnataka's trusted solar EPC company

Samson Solar Energy is a leading solar EPC (Engineering, Procurement & Construction) company with 15+ years of experience delivering high-quality solar power solutions across Karnataka. As an MNRE Category A channel partner and ISO-certified company, we specialize in the design, supply, installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance of rooftop solar power systems for residential, commercial, industrial, educational, healthcare and government customers.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help customers reduce electricity costs while contributing to a cleaner, more sustainable future through renewable energy.

Our Vision

To become Karnataka's most trusted solar energy company by providing innovative, reliable and affordable renewable energy solutions.
