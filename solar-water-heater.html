How it works

Sunlight in, hot water out

Solar collectors absorb solar radiation and transfer the heat to water. The heated water is stored in an insulated tank, which helps retain the heat for later use. In thermosiphon systems, circulation happens naturally without requiring a circulation pump — so properly sized and installed systems keep hot water available throughout the day.
